How your radar works

Only verified posts less than 72 hours old are saved. After 72 hours in your shortlist, roles you haven’t applied to become inactive. Applied and interviewed roles become ghosted after 14 days without an update. Rejected roles and offers stay as you set them. Use Check in to record an update without changing status.
